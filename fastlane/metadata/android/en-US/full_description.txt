Kodomo is a parental-control fork of Mihon, an Android reader for manga, webtoons and comics.

What is different:
* Extensions rated 18+ or mixed cannot be installed, loaded or listed. This is fixed at build time — there is no setting that changes it.
* Extension stores must be on a build-time allowlist, and adding one requires a parent PIN that is separate from the device's screen lock.
* Everything else is Mihon: customizable reader, categories, library updates, tracker sync, offline downloads, backups, Material You theming.

Limits worth knowing: extensions declare their own content rating, so a source marked safe can still carry mature titles. This filters extensions, not content, and is not a substitute for supervision or for Android's own parental controls.

This application hosts zero content and has no affiliation with any content provider. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by the Mihon project.
